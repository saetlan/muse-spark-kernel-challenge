### Blocked (with the reason)

| ID | Idea | Why blocked |
|---|---|---|
| X1 | Native FP4 tensor cores (Gemini's point 1) | Hardware: Hopper (sm_90) has no FP4 tensor-core instructions; those are Blackwell only. |
| X2 | FP8 × FP8 tensor cores (FP4 codes fit exactly in FP8) | Rules: activations must stay BF16 wherever SGLang keeps them BF16. Its Marlin keeps them BF16. |
| X4 | Multiply by the top-k routing weight inside the second matrix multiply (TensorRT-LLM's Marlin `mul_topk_weights=True`) | Math: Muse normalizes each expert's output (RMSNorm) before weighting, and the norm would cancel a weight applied earlier. The weight must come after the norm. |
| X5 | Pre-combine block scale × global scale (× decode bias) into FP32 scales stored in `prepare()` (suggested in R-G) | Rules: weights stay NVFP4, repacked or compressed only losslessly and "smaller". FP32 scales are 4× larger and a different format, and would also read more bytes. Keep E4M3 scales and combine in the kernel. |
| X3 | Latent Sparse Attention (LSA) or any sparse/top-k attention (your link, Nov 3) | Kernel rules: attend to every key, "no top-k, sparse or block-skipping attention". Serving rules: the model must stay the same, and LSA needs a newly trained indexer; outputs must match stock.
